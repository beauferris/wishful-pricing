Privacy Policy — Wishful Pricing

Last updated: June 3, 2026
Contact: h-beauferris@hotmail.com

Overview

Wishful Pricing is a Shopify app that lets merchants configure product discounts based on size, quantity, and optional line-item options. The app runs inside the Shopify admin and at checkout via Shopify Functions.

Data we access

With merchant permission (OAuth scopes), the app may access:

  • Discount configuration stored in Shopify metafields on discounts
  • Product and variant data to show pricing settings and sync storefront metafields
  • Shop metafields for a pricing catalog used by the theme

The app does not require customer personal data beyond what Shopify already processes for checkout and orders.

Data stored

  • Configuration is stored in Shopify metafields on the merchant’s shop (discount, product, and shop resources).
  • Theme pricing previews read public storefront metafields defined by the app.

Data sharing

We do not sell merchant or customer data. Data stays within Shopify’s platform except where Shopify processes it as part of normal store operations.

Hosting

This app is extension-only: discount logic runs on Shopify’s infrastructure. We do not operate a separate database for merchant catalog data.

Merchant responsibilities

Merchants are responsible for their store’s privacy policy, customer notices, and lawful use of any customer information they collect through their store.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do.
