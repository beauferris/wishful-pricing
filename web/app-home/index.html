Wishful Pricing

Custom size, quantity, and volume pricing for print-on-demand and made-to-size products. Checkout discounts run through Shopify Functions so cart totals match your product-page calculator.

1. Configure pricing (admin)

  1. Open Discounts in Shopify admin.
  2. Create or edit an automatic discount that uses Wishful Pricing.
  3. On the discount details page, use the Wishful pricing card.
  4. Choose products, set rate tables, then click Save pricing.

2. Show prices on the storefront (theme editor)

  1. Go to Online Store → Themes → Customize.
  2. Open a Product template.
  3. In the main product section, click Add block.
  4. Under Apps, add Wishful pricing.
  5. Save. Only products saved in step 1 will show the calculator.

Uninstalling the app removes app blocks from the theme (no leftover Liquid in theme files).

3. After changes

If you delete a discount or remove products, open any Wishful discount and use Refresh storefront to rebuild the shop catalog and clear stale product pages.
